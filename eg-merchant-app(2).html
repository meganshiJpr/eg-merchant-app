Common questions
Money deducted but transaction failed?
Failed transaction amounts are auto-refunded to your wallet within 24–48 hours. Check Transactions for the refund entry. If not received after 48 hours, raise a ticket with the transaction ID.
Manual deposit not credited yet?
Deposits are verified against our bank statement, usually within 30 minutes during working hours. Keep your UTR number ready if you need to contact support.
How to change settlement bank account?
Go to Profile → Bank account → Change settlement account. New accounts are verified within 1 working day. Settlements continue to the old account until verification completes.
Forgot transaction PIN?
Go to Profile, then Change PIN. If you don't remember the current PIN, use Forgot PIN on that screen to reset via OTP on your registered mobile number.
